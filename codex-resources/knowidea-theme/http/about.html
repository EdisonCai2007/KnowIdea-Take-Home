Our Story

KNOWIDEA was born from firsthand experience with the frustrations of modern business intelligence. Our founders spent years at the intersection of data, strategy, and decision-making: Yatharth at TD, Manulife, and Mercer, Brian at AWS. They watched brilliant leaders struggle with the same problem.

They saw executives drowning in dashboards yet starving for insight. Teams spending weeks on analyses that arrived too late to matter. Decisions made on gut feeling because the data couldn't keep up with the pace of business.

So they asked: What if AI could bridge the gap between raw data and strategic action? What if every employee could have an intelligent co-worker that understands context, anticipates needs, and delivers answers in the moment they're needed?
